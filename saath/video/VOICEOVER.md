# Voiceover script (optional)

The video ships with music, interface sounds and on-screen subtitles. To add a voice, record
these lines (calm, warm, unhurried; Indian English) and lay them over `saath-prototype.mp4`
at the times shown. The subtitles in `video.html` (`SUBS`) use the same text and timing.

| 0:30 | Priya books Ravi, a verified local helper, and pays with UPI. |
