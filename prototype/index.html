01 · Journey search

Plan from
where you are.

  • Google Places autocomplete with session tokens and debounced typing
  • Current location, swap, recent and saved journeys
  • Every place resolved to exact coordinates before routing
02 · Traffic intelligence

Compare routes
with live traffic.

  • Routes API TRAFFIC_AWARE_OPTIMAL with alternatives
  • Congestion drawn only from traffic-on-polyline data
  • Honest labels: “fastest among available alternatives”
  • A freshness timestamp on every result
03 · Intelligent stops

Stops ranked by the
detour you’ll drive.

  • Search Along Route on your selected route polyline
  • You pick the category — no blanket searches
  • 300 m from the highway can still mean +17 minutes of real driving
04 · Detour engine

Origin → stop →
destination, re-routed.

  • Added time = route with stop − baseline, under the same traffic assumptions
  • Detailed detours only for top candidates — cost under control
  • Driving time only. Time spent at the stop is excluded — and labelled
05 · Multi-stop journeys

Your stops.
Your order.

  • Add, remove and reorder stops with intermediate waypoints
  • Updated ETA, distance and added journey time
  • Order is never changed silently — optimise only on request
06 · Emergency hub

Help when
it matters.

  • 112 opens your dialer — you place the call, never automatic
  • Works offline, independent of maps or network
  • Location shared only when you tap Share
  • Nearby hospitals without unverified capacity claims
07 · Cost & privacy

Smart about
every request.

  • Deduplication, cancellation and request fingerprints
  • Field masks and per-session API budgets
  • No background tracking. No ads. No login.
